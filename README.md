# DEMONIC

Demonic is a fast-paced hack 'n' slash experience where lightning-fast reflexes and sharp intuition are your only lifelines. Inspired by the adrenaline-fueled combat of Metal Gear Rising and the stylish chaos of Devil May Cry, you’ll wield a powerful Psi-Sword and unleash devastating demonic abilities. Step into the role of humanity’s last hope and carve your path through hordes of relentless enemies - The Fate of the World burns at the Edge of your Blade.

---

## 🔧 Features

- ⚔️ Combat Chaos – Every slash, strike, and skill ramps up your combo counter, unlocking devastating finishers. The higher your style, the deadlier you become—stay cool, stay sharp, and don’t break the chain.

- 🚀 Movement is Mayhem – Walls, gaps, and gravity? Just obstacles waiting to be shattered. Chain triple jumps, wall-runs, and pinpoint dashes—even double dashes—to tear through the terrain and kite your enemies.

- 🎯Precision or Punishment – Time your parry to turn defense into offense. Hit that perfect frame for a Perfect Parry that shatters their rhythm. Slip through death with a Perfect Dodge, then strike where it hurts. Mistime it? You bleed. Nail it? They break.

- ✨Unholy Gifts – You’re more than human now. Steal vitality with every cut, siphoning life essence from your enemies to restore your own. Channel dark energy into the earth and unleash a shockwave that hurls foes into the void. Your powers aren’t just weapons—they’re a curse reborn as strength.

- ☢️Demonic Frenzy -  When rage takes over, the veil shatters.  Your slashes hit like a wrecking ball, and the battlefield becomes a slaughterhouse. In this awakened state, you don’t just fight—you hunt.

- 🎮 Best played with a controller for that ergonomic control layout and haptic feedback effect.
